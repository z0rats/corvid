---
title: Newsfeed
description: Aggregated cybersecurity news with automatic IOC extraction.
---

Aggregates articles from trusted sources (Wired, The Hacker News, Security Magazine, Threatpost,
TechCrunch Security, Dark Reading, and more) so you can stay on top of industry trends and
emerging threats without visiting each site individually. IOCs and MITRE ATT&CK techniques are
extracted from articles automatically, and AI-assisted analysis helps you triage what matters.

Each configured feed tracks its own last-fetch/last-success/last-error state, so a persistently
failing feed surfaces a warning in the UI instead of only appearing in logs.
